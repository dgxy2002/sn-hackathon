Be protected again and again
Built to cover more, so you can worry less
When cancer strikes, the last thing you should have to worry about is whether
you have enough protection to stay financially resilient. With up to S$700,000
coverage, you can focus on your recovery, while we take care of the rest.
Not a once-and-done plan
Should cancer return, we believe your protection should too. Get 100%
of the coverage amount each time for not only the first diagnosis, but also
up to two major stage relapses of major cancer, with each relapse claimable
two years apart2.
The smarter way to stay covered
Choose between a coverage period of 20 years, or till 65 years old. Whichever
you prefer, you can plan ahead with clarity, knowing your premiums stay the
same every year1. By starting early, you can pay lower premiums based on your
current age for more affordable long-term protection.
One add-on cover for 360° protection
The optional Enhanced Cancer Protect Plus widens your safety net by including
early, intermediate and catastrophic cancer coverage and increasing your total
coverage up to 400%, so nothing ever catches you off guard.
