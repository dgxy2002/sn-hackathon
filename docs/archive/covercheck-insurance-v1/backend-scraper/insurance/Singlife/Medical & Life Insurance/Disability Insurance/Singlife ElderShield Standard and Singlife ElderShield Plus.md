What’s severe disability1?
A person is considered severely disabled
and in need of long-term care when they
can’t independently perform at least three
of the six Activities of Daily Living (ADLs).

What’s the basic ElderShield payout?
In the event of severe disability, you can get a basic payout from your ElderShield
policy. The government’s ElderShield scheme gives a payout when an individual is
severely disabled, i.e. unable to perform three or more ADLs.
