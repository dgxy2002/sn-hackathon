Important Notes
Please refer to the Product Summary for more details.
1. 20% No Claims Discount on Singlife Health Plus applies if:
• The cover start date or renewal date of your policy is on or after 1 Apr 2025.
• Total claims we have paid during the assessment period under your Singlife Shield and Singlife Health Plus policies (excluding MediShield Life) is S$0.
Assessment period refers to the following periods starting two months before your next policy renewal date:
• First policy year: Past 10 months
• Second policy year: Past 22 months
• Third policy year onwards: Past 24 months
Any refund (e.g. refunds from other insurance policies that covers the same Life Assured), excluding the refund amount for MediShield Life, which we receive during an assessment period
will reduce the total claim amount paid in that assessment period.
2. Discounted premium rates for child(ren) refers to Family Discount for Child(ren):
• Singlife Shield Plan 2
- Up to 4 children, until 20 Age Next Birthday (ANB). Both parents have to be covered under Singlife Shield Plan 1 or Singlife Shield Plan 2.
• Singlife Health Plus Public
- Up to 4 children, until 20 Age Next Birthday (ANB). Your dependent child who is covered under Singlife Shield’s family discount for child(ren) is also eligible for free cover under
Singlife Health Plus Public, attached to Singlife Shield Plan 2. Both parents have to be covered under Singlife Shield Plan 1 or 2, together with either of these (Singlife Health Plus options
-Private/Public Lite, Private Cover, Private/Public Prime or Private/Public).
3. Free newborn benefit:
• Starts from the date a newborn child turns 15 days old, on the day of discharge from hospital after birth, whichever is later.
• Coverage ends on the date the newborn is 6 months old or takes up a MediSave-approved Integrated Shield Plan, whichever is earlier.
• Both parents must be covered under Singlife Shield Plan 1 or Singlife Shield Plan 2 continuously for at least 10 months from the cover start date of their respective policies on the
newborn’s date of birth.
Coverage excludes pre-hospital and post-hospital treatments outside of the coverage period.
4. This benefit is claimable once per lifetime and provides coverage of up to S$20,000 per lifetime, for a maximum of 730 consecutive days. This benefit is only for Life Assured with both
Singlife Health Plus and Singlife CareShield/ElderShield Standard/Plus, who has been certified eligible to claim for Severe Disability Benefit.
5. Preferred medical provider(s) refers to the list of approved medical providers listed at: https://singlife.com/medicalspecialists (updated periodically).
6. Pre-hospital treatment benefit: Covers eligible medical expenses incurred up to 180 days before admission (Plans 1 – 3) at A&E and preferred medical provider(s).
7. Post-hospital treatment benefit: Covers eligible medical expenses incurred up to 365 days after discharge (Plans 1 – 3) at A&E and preferred medical provider(s).
8. Only cancer drug treatments listed on the Cancer Drug List (CDL) are covered under the Outpatient Cancer Drug Treatment benefit. Outpatient Cancer Drug Services that are part of
a cancer drug treatment (including treatments not on the CDL), are claimable under the Outpatient Cancer Drug Services benefit.
9. To view the latest version of the Cancer Drug List, which covers majority of the Cancer Drug Treatments approved by the Health Sciences Authority (HSA).
Visit https://go.gov.sg/moh-cancerdruglist for more information (updated periodically by the Ministry of Health (MOH)).
10. Extra Inpatient Benefit for 5 critical illnesses, covering heart attack of specified severity, major cancer, stroke, end-stage lung disease and end-stage liver disease.
