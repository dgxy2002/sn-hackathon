Features
Key Features
Guaranteed renewability
FWD Life PA ensures peace of mind with guaranteed policy renewals1
until the person insured reaches age 100. Your coverage remains
active as long as premiums are paid on time, securing long-term
protection for you and your family.
Lifetime 10% No Claim Discount
Enjoy a lifetime 10% No Claim Discount on your premiums after three
consecutive claim-free years2. This discount applies to the entire
policy,including all optional riders. Once applied, this discount is
perpetual, helping you save while staying protected.
Comprehensive medical coverage
FWD Life PA allows you to claim for both inpatient and outpatient
medical expenses resulting from accidents3, covering hospital stays,
outpatient treatments, visits to Chinese physicians, chiropractors and
acupuncturists.
Unparalleled coverage
Get covered with 300% of sum insured when travelling as a fare-
paying passenger on a commercial flight or cruise. Enjoy coverage of
200% of sum insured when you are a passenger on public or private
transport or while you’re using personal mobility devices (PMD)4.

Our Catch-All Rider5adds an extra layer of financial security. If any injury

Supplement your base plan with the Income Support Rider to receive daily

Important to know
Important notes
FWD Life PA is underwritten by FWD Singapore Pte. Ltd. (FWD), [Reg. No. 200501737H].
There are certain conditions such as pre-existing conditions and exclusions, whereby no benefits will be payable.
If you aren’t completely satisfied with your policy, and you haven’t made a claim under it, you have 14 calendar
days from the date you receive your policy to cancel it.
Please refer to the policy contract for more details on the product benefits.
1. Exceptions may apply. In compliance with laws, regulations and requests of relevant authorities in the areas of
sanctions, anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing, we may take steps that include ending
your policy and related cover, as well as treating premiums paid and amounts payable under your policy, in
any manner we deem appropriate.
2. The NCD is applied on the premiums payable for each person insured, and not on the entire policy. If you and
your children are insured under the same policy, each person must qualify separately for the NCD. Different
persons insured under the policy may or may not qualify for the NCD.
3. We will reimburse reasonable and necessary medical expenses incurred for the person insured’s inpatient and
outpatient treatment asa result of an accident occurring in or outside of Singapore, up to the benefit limit
payable for each accident. All medical expenses must be incurred within 12 months from the date of the
accident.
4. 300% of sum insured pay out refers to the TripleIndemnity on Accidental Death and Disability Benefit
(ADDB). We will pay triple the benefit amount under the ADDB if the accidental death or permanent disability
was sustained while the person insured was travelling as a fare-paying passenger on a commercial plane or on
a cruise ship (with at least a night’s stay on board the cruise ship) operated by a licensed operator.
200% of sum insured cover refers to the DoubleIndemnity on ADDB. We will pay double the benefit amount
under the ADDB if the accidental death or permanent disability was sustained while the person insured was:
a) a passenger on public transport;
b) a passenger or driver in a private transport;
c) a pedestrian where such loss is caused by public transport, private transport or a personal mobility device;
d) using a personal mobility device; or
e) a pedestrian (including as a passenger in a public lift, other than lifts in mines and construction sites); or
f) caught in a fire which started at home, in a theatre, hotel, public auditorium, school, hospital or shopping
mall. Factories are not covered.
For person(s) insured below the age of 18, we will also pay double the benefit amount under the ADDB if the
accidental death or permanent disability was sustained while the person insured was:
a) within school premises on a school day or participating in school activities organised and supervised by the
school authorities;
b) participating in school activities organised and supervised by the school authorities outside of school
premises; or
c) travelling as a passenger on a school bus, private bus or excursion bus to and from the school or a venue
where the school activity(ies) is/are taking/took place.
5. We will pay an additional 10% of the ADDB sum insured if the person insured had an accident that requires an
Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Stay for 120 or more consecutive hours with the use of invasive life support, directly
and solely as a result of that accident. This benefit will be paid once per life.
